05 / Personal reflection

What I learned

Studying poetry through Padgett (1987) and Abdallah et al. (2024) helped me understand that a poem is not simply a text with beautiful words or a fixed structure. Form shapes how an idea is experienced, while imagery and symbolism invite readers to look beyond the literal meaning. I also realized that interpretation depends on context: a natural image may express freedom, longing, or a cultural belief, depending on the poem and its time. As a future English teacher, I would encourage learners to explore poems by listening to their rhythm, noticing vivid images, and sharing the personal and cultural meanings they discover. This can make poetry less intimidating and more connected to real human experiences.

Poetry connects personal emotion with ideas that can travel across cultures and generations.
